MONOLITH

MONOLITH is a real-time generative visual engine. Everything you're about to see was generated live, in a single pass, from one source and driven entirely by the sound — the machine composes, cuts and reacts frame by frame. Nothing is pre-rendered or edited. This is one raw example.
